# 🧭 **Customer Call Center Analytics — Executive Intelligence Suite (Final Spec)**

## 🏠 PURPOSE

Create a unified AI-driven control system that gives leadership **real-time foresight** into:

* **Customer health and retention**
* **Operational efficiency and compliance**
* **Portfolio risk and financial impact**
* **Predictive actions and ROI outcomes**

---

## ⚙️ CORE STRUCTURE — 3-TAB EXECUTIVE VIEW

| Tab                        | Role                    | Leadership Focus                                            | Primary Question                                               |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Control Tower**          | Executive Summary       | Overall business health, portfolio risk, financial outcomes | *“Are we on plan — and what should we do now?”*                |
| **Servicing Ops**          | COO / Head of Servicing | SLA, FCR, staffing, delinquency, compliance                 | *“Can we keep our servicing promises and efficiency targets?”* |
| **Marketing Intelligence** | CMO / Retention Lead    | Refi, churn, referral, segment ROI                          | *“How can we grow or retain more customers profitably?”*       |

---

## 🧠 TAB 1: CONTROL TOWER (Leadership Cockpit)

### **Key Goals**

* Aggregate business KPIs into one **“at-a-glance” view.**
* Blend **financial**, **operational**, and **customer** signals.
* Drive **strategic actions** through forecasts and ROI context.

### **Core Components**

#### 🧾 KPI Row (Top Summary)

| Metric                     | Description                                       | Source                             |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| **Customer Health Index**  | Composite score (FCR, CSAT, churn, delinquency)   | Derived from Servicing + Marketing |
| **Revenue Momentum**       | $ lift from refi, retention, and referral actions | Marketing AI                       |
| **Operational Efficiency** | SLA %, cases resolved per FTE, cost per loan      | Servicing Ops                      |
| **Compliance Posture**     | % audits passed, open exceptions                  | Compliance Feed                    |

➡ Each KPI shows **trend (↑/↓)** and **variance vs plan.**

#### 📈 Forecast Outlook

* Prophet + LLM hybrid: 4-hour to 30-day forecast.
* Display **top predicted deviations** (e.g., “FCR trending red in hardship queue”).
* Confidence band + potential dollar impact.

#### 💼 Financial Integration

Add **P&L layer**:

* Servicing margin = revenue – cost per loan.
* Reserve exposure vs forecast loss.
* Cost of delinquency (unrecovered interest + call handling cost).

#### 🔔 Action Queue

Ranked by ROI + urgency:

1. Approve staffing reallocation ($ impact)
2. Launch retention campaign
3. Review compliance exception

Each card:
→ *Action → Risk / ROI → Confidence → Responsible team.*

#### 📊 Alerts & Exceptions

Top 3 red metrics:

* “Delinquency > target by +0.6%”
* “FCR predicted below SLA in hardship queue”
* “AI forecast drift detected – review model health”

#### 💡 Executive Narrative Summary

> *“Customer Health Index stable at 82.3. Predicted drop in FCR next 4h could cost $40K if unaddressed. Marketing lift +18% WoW. Compliance fully green.”*
